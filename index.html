Safety & privacy
CareTrack is a record-keeping tool, not a diagnostic or emergency service. The current GitHub Pages version stores entries locally in this browser. Avoid entering real patient information until secure clinical infrastructure is implemented.
CareTrack MVP. For production use, real patient data requires secure authentication, encryption, access controls, audit logs, validated clinical rules, consent/privacy workflows and applicable legal/regulatory review.
